## About

Hey, I'm Samuel 👋 I'm a Software Engineering student, and I like the moment when something I built leaves my machine and starts solving a real problem for real people.

I got hooked on automation during my internship at **HM Solucoes**. There I built N8N flows that took repetitive work off people's plates: WhatsApp-integrated conversations that cut client response time, and Google Sheets pipelines that kept data organized without anyone copying and pasting. That's when I realized I wanted to build things that change how a team works.

At **FLS Consultoria** I took that much further. I went from flows to full systems: I designed, built and maintain the company's lead automation stack in Node.js. A lead API, a WhatsApp assistant and an admin dashboard run in production every day, connected to the CRM, Meta Ads, payments, calendars and e-mail. I also manage the company's paid traffic, so I get to see both sides: the ad that brings the lead and the system that turns it into a meeting or a sale. I build with AI as a pair programmer, but I keep the engineering solid: tests, security and clean integrations.

My path started in IT support, and today I also work as an IT Support Analyst at Penso Tecnologia. That background gave me a feel for infrastructure that I carry into everything I build.

When I'm not coding, I'm studying AWS and Cybersecurity, playing with Docker and distributed systems, or listening to music on Spotify 🎧. My goal is to understand the whole path, from the cable in the wall to the API in the cloud.

## 🚀 Featured: lead automation in production

At **FLS Consultoria** I designed and shipped the company's lead automation stack: three Node.js systems integrated with **12 services**. They replaced manual work across the whole sales funnel, from the ad click to the booked meeting or the approved purchase. I built them with **AI-assisted development (Claude Code)** and backed them with 408 automated tests, a security review and LGPD compliance.

> These three systems run daily at a real company. The code was anonymized for publication ("Acme", `example.com`), and the logic is the same as in production.

| Repository | What it does | Tests |
|---|---|---|
| 📥 [**lead-capture-api**](https://github.com/samis7th/lead-capture-api) | Captures landing page leads into the CRM (Monday.com), sends conversions to the Meta Conversions API and processes Hotmart payment webhooks. Queue-based, so **no lead is lost** even when the CRM is down. | 61 |
| 💬 [**whatsapp-assistant**](https://github.com/samis7th/whatsapp-assistant) | WhatsApp pre-sales assistant (ManyChat): qualifies leads by revenue, books meetings (Calendly + Google Calendar), sends WhatsApp and e-mail reminders and runs a weekly mentoring raffle. | 306 |
| 📊 [**admin-dashboard**](https://github.com/samis7th/admin-dashboard) | Team dashboard: real-time leads, charts, meetings, raffle management and live health of every integration. Secure login, demo mode. | 41 |

**Measurable impact**
- ⚡ Form confirmation went from **10–15 s to instant**. During a CRM outage, **zero signups were lost**.
- 🤝 One automated WhatsApp campaign reached **412 subscribers** with no manual sending.
- 💰 Every sale reaches the CRM with its ad campaign of origin, so the return of each channel is measurable.
- ⏱️ Data entry, reminders, group checks, raffles and alerts are no longer done by hand, so the team can focus on meetings and sales.

**Integrations:** Monday.com · Meta Conversions API · Hotmart · WhatsApp/ManyChat · Instagram · Calendly · Google Calendar · Brevo · Telegram · MySQL · Squarespace · Hostinger
